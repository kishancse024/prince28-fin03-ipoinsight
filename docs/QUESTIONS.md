# Real user questions: IPOInsight

Written the way my user types them. User: Retail investor in Trichy who applies for 2-3 IPOs a month and wants to understand the risks first.

These 10 questions become my first evaluation set in Week 3. Question 6 is not answerable from my documents: the product must say "I don't know".

| # | Question | Type | Where the answer is (document, page) | More than one page? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is a DRHP and how is it different from an RHP? | simple | **SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018** (Reg 2(1) / Chapter II) &amp; **DRHP/RHP Cover Page / Definitions** | No |
| 2 | How much of [Company X]'s IPO is an offer for sale? | simple | **DRHP / RHP of [Company X]**, Cover Page / Section III: "The Issue" | No |
| 3 | What are the top 5 risk factors listed by [Company X]? | simple | **DRHP / RHP of [Company X]**, Section III: "Risk Factors" (Internal Risk Factors 1–5) | No |
| 4 | I am choosing between two IPOs opening next week. Compare their use of proceeds, revenue growth, debt and promoter holding after the issue, with page citations. | multi-step (several pages) | **DRHPs / RHPs of both companies**: "Objects of the Offer", "Financial Information", "Capital Structure" | Yes |
| 5 | Compare the ICDR rules for mainboard IPO anchor investors and retail allocation. | comparison | **SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018**: Regulation 6, Regulation 32, &amp; Schedule XIII (Part A) | Yes |
| 6 | Will this IPO list at a premium? | unanswerable: must reply "I don't know" | none: not in any document | - |
| 7 | Has the company been making profits consistently over the last three financial years?| simple | **DRHP / RHP of [Company X]**, Section VI: "Financial Information" (Restated Statement of Profit &amp; Loss) | Yes |
| 8 | Does the company have any ongoing legal cases or major pending litigations? | simple |**DRHP / RHP of [Company X]**, Section IX: "Outstanding Litigation and Material Developments"| No |
| 9 | Is the company planning to use the IPO money to repay existing loans or expand its business?| simple | **DRHP / RHP of [Company X]**, Section III: "Objects of the Offer"| No |
| 10 | 	Are there any related-party transactions that investors should be aware of?| multi-step (several pages) |**DRHP / RHP of [Company X]**, Section VI: "Financial Information" (Notes to Restated Financial Statements - Related Party Disclosures)| Yes |
